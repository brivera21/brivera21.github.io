# Dr. Brian Rivera

**Visiting Assistant Professor**  
Department of Psychology, St. Olaf College

My research uses EEG and fMRI to investigate the neural mechanisms of mathematical thinking, with particular emphasis on numerical cognition and fraction processing. I run the Root Lab at St. Olaf College, where undergraduate researchers help carry out this work.
