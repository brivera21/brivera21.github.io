How each lesson works

  • The lesson is on the left and a practice window on the right. The practice window copies the MATLAB desktop: Current Folder, Workspace, Command Window, and in lessons 7 and 8 the Editor.
  • Every example has a Run button, or you can type it yourself. Typing is better practice.
  • "Your turn" at the end lists a few things to do. Each one ticks itself when you do it.
  • "Start over" resets the practice window. Nothing is saved between visits.

What the practice window is, and is not

It is a small model of MATLAB that runs in your browser. It understands the commands in these lessons, gives MATLAB's error messages for the common mistakes, and has a pretend computer with an EEGLAB folder and three datasets. The data are simulated: 32 channels, 60 seconds at 500 Hz. It does not draw plots or open the EEGLAB window, and pop_eegfiltnew records the filter without changing the numbers.

To follow along in real MATLAB, download EEGLAB from the EEGLAB download page, unzip it into your Documents/MATLAB folder, and start at lesson 3 to put it on the path. Your version number will differ from the eeglab2025.1 used here.
