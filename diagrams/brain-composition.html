Brain Composition Breakdown

The volume inside the skull is fixed. Here is a breakdown of what constitutes that volume.

1  ·  A fixed container

The volume inside the adult skull is fixed at about 1.4 to 1.7 litres of rigid, non-expandable space. This is the Monro-Kellie doctrine: brain tissue, blood and cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) together fill a constant volume, so if one grows, another must shrink or pressure rises. At the coarsest level that split is roughly brain tissue 80%, blood 10%, CSF 10%.

Composition by volume

Breaking the 80% brain tissue into its parts. “Neurons” here means the cell bodies plus all their wiring (axons, dendrites and synapses, the neuropil), which is what actually takes up the space.

2  ·  The ~86 billion neurons, by type & where they live

There are about 86 billion neurons in the brain. This is a more accurate estimate of the 100 billion often cited. These neurons break down into the following types of neurons.

The 86 billion above counts only neurons inside the brain. Beyond it, the rest of the nervous system adds roughly another 1.5 to 2 billion:
Spinal cord~1 billionmotor, sensory relay & interneurons down the cord
Enteric (gut) nervous system~400 to 600 millionthe “second brain” lining the gut wall
Peripheral & autonomic gangliahundreds of millionsdorsal-root, sympathetic & sensory ganglia
So the whole nervous system holds on the order of ~88 billion neurons. The brain is about 98% of them, but not all.
