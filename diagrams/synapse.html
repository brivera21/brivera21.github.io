The Synapse

A neuron passes its signal to the next cell by releasing a chemical. The sequence runs in five steps, and almost every drug that changes behavior works by interfering with one of them.

Excitatory: the channel lets Na⁺ in, the cell depolarizes, and it moves closer to threshold.

Where drugs act

A ligand is any substance that binds a receptor. A drug that mimics or strengthens a transmitter is an agonist; one that blocks or weakens it is an antagonist.
