The Neuron

A corticospinal (Betz-type) layer-5 pyramidal neuron. Its cell body and dendrites span only about 2 mm, a speck at the axon's true scale. The axon runs the full height of the frame, about 1 meter, from motor cortex down the corticospinal tract to the lumbosacral spinal cord.
