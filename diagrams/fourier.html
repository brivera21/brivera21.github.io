Try this

  1. On the two tones preset, drag the winding frequency slowly from 0 to 20 Hz. The pink dot stays near the center except at 3 and 7 Hz, where it swings out. Those are the two waves you built.
  2. Set wave 2 to 0 amplitude. One peak. Put it back and move its frequency; the second peak follows it.
  3. Choose the close pair (8 and 9 Hz) and drag the window from 2 s down to 0.5 s. The two peaks merge into one, because a window of length T cannot separate frequencies closer than 1/T.
  4. Turn the noise up with the alpha preset. The peak survives noise that hides the rhythm completely in the time plot.
