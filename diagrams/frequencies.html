Frequencies

Comparing 1 Hz against 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 20, 50 and 100 Hz. All waves are shown over the same 1-second window. Frequency (f) is the number of complete cycles per second; the period T = 1/f is how long one cycle takes. Doubling frequency halves the period.

Overlay

All waves on one time axis. 1 Hz is the reference and is always shown. Toggle the others on or off to compare, or sweep the sliders to try any frequency from 1 to 20 Hz at any amplitude. Hover for exact values, and press play to watch a point ride each wave. High frequencies look like dense bands over a full second, so use the window buttons to zoom in on them.
